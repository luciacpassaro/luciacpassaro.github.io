# Research

My research centres on **Natural Language Processing** and related AI technologies, with a focus on language models, semantics, multimodality, and domain-specific applications.

I am especially interested in the development and evaluation of NLP systems for real-world scenarios, including misinformation detection, affective computing, information extraction, legal NLP, domain adaptation, and intelligent document analysis.

## Research areas

- **Trustworthy NLP and responsible AI**
- **Language resources and evaluation**
- **Semantics and Interpretability**
- **Domain adaptation, continual learning, and multimodality**
- **NLP and LLMs for Software Engineering**

## Selected projects with leading roles

<details open>
<summary><strong>CLMs4BPO — Conditional Language Models for Business Process Optimization</strong></summary>

**Scientific Coordinator.**  
Project funded by Regione Toscana for the activation of research fellowships.

The project focused on the automatic generation of text conditioned by explicit metadata, using generative AI techniques and instruction-tuned Large Language Models.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><strong>PRIN 2022 — Psychologically-tailored approaches to Debunk Fake News</strong></summary>

**Work Package Leader.**  
The project studies fake news recognition and personalized debunking strategies based on the psychological and behavioural characteristics of individuals.

The work combines misinformation detection, NLP, and user-aware approaches to debunking.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><strong>SEM — Sem Il Chattadino</strong></summary>

**Scientific Coordinator.**  
Project funded by Regione Toscana POR CreO FESR 2014–2020.

The project aimed at developing a chatbot for the Public Administration domain, bringing together CoLing Lab and partner companies.

</details>

## Ongoing and recent research projects

<details>
<summary><strong>EMERGE — Emergent Awareness from Minimal Collectives</strong></summary>

European project funded under the EIC programme.

The project investigates emergent forms of collaborative awareness in collectives of minimal artificial agents, focusing on how simple agents can develop representations of each other, their environment, and cooperative behaviours.

My contribution concerns language-model updating and multimodal communication in virtual agents.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>FAIR — Future Artificial Intelligence Research</strong></summary>

The project addresses research questions, methodologies, models, technologies, and ethical and legal aspects for building Artificial Intelligence systems.

My contribution is connected to **Spoke 1**, coordinated by the University of Pisa, with a focus on **Human-Centered Lifelong Learning for Complex Data**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>TAILOR — Foundations of Trustworthy AI</strong></summary>

European H2020 project on Trustworthy AI, integrating reasoning, learning, and optimization.

The consortium promotes a human-centred vision of responsible AI based on expertise, cooperation, and diversity.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>PRIN 2022 PNRR — New Frontiers for a Participatory Criminal Justice Towards the Thresholds of Metaverse</strong></summary>

The project investigates the impact of ICT technologies on judicial decision-making and on the conduct of remote legal proceedings.

</details>
